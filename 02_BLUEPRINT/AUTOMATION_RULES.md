# Automation Rules

## Command Center
- Surface overdue/blocked work.
- Surface today's publishing work from content dates.
- Surface upcoming renewals.
- Surface unbilled eligible expenses.

## Content
**C1:** approval updates lifecycle, review record, activity and downstream publishing readiness.

**C2:** change request updates lifecycle, preserves comments and exposes execution work.

**C3:** adding a publishing date automatically updates calendar projection.

## Billing
**B1:** billing event generates draft from recurring charges + eligible tool expenses.

**B2:** an expense cannot be invoiced twice.

**B3:** unpaid invoice after due date becomes OVERDUE and appears in attention queue.

## Renewals
**R1:** renewal approaching creates reminder linked to client/tool. V1 baseline: 48-hour alert.

## Meetings
**M1:** Fathom context creates proposed dates/tasks/content ideas/decisions.

**M2:** confirmed proposals can create/update canonical records and activity.

## Credentials
**S1:** reveal/copy requires permission and creates an audit event; secret never enters logs.

## General
Automate low-risk bookkeeping. Propose medium-risk changes. Require explicit authorization for sensitive external or financial mutations.
