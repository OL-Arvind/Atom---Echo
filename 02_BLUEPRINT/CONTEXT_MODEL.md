# Context Model

## Why
The OS should remember information the agency repeatedly needs about clients instead of repeatedly asking operators to restate it.

## Categories
- strategy: positioning, audience, goals, offer, priorities;
- voice/content: tone, pillars, liked/rejected examples;
- operational preferences: review style, communication, cadence;
- decisions;
- history;
- evidence: approved/rejected work and performance evidence.

## Authority
Explicit client statements > approved decisions > confirmed meeting actions > observed operational data > inferred patterns.

Inferences must not silently become facts.

## Lifecycle
`observed/entered → stored with source → reused → superseded → history retained`

## Phase 1
Establish context storage and meeting-to-context flow. Advanced autonomous reasoning is later.
