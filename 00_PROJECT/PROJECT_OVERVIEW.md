1. **Command Center**: Action-first morning surface showing what requires attention right now.
2. **Master Content Engine & Projected Calendar**: Unified publishing pipeline across all clients.
3. **Zero-Friction Review Portal**: WhatsApp magic-token link to lightweight mobile PWA for 5-second client approvals.
4. **Automated Invoicing & Pass-Through Tool Billing**: Retainers + client SaaS costs (HeyReach, Clay) automatically rolled into monthly invoices.

7. **Fathom Meeting Context**: Weekly strategy calls parsed into actionable operational proposals.
